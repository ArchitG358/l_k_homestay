Where you'll stay

In the heart of Ujjain, close to the Jyotirlinga.

Ujjain is one of India's oldest cities and home to the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga — one of the twelve Jyotirlingas in the country, and the reason most guests come here. L&K Homestay sits in Sunrise City, a residential pocket a short ride from the temple, the newly built Mahakal Lok corridor, and the ghats along the Kshipra river.

  • A short ride to Mahakaleshwar Temple & Mahakal Lok
  • Close to the Kshipra river ghats
  • Quiet residential lane, easy for pre-dawn Bhasma Aarti starts
  • Local auto & cab stands nearby for onward travel
